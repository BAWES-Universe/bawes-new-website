# Hero (Current)

Source: `app/page.tsx`, `components/home/Plaza.tsx`.

- Eyebrow: People and AI, living together
- Headline: **A Universe where you belong.** ("Universe" carries the brand gradient)
- Lead: Walk in, meet people and make a space of your own. **Build anything** with Brick, the AI that lives here with you, and connect it to the Apps you already use.
- Buttons: Enter the Universe (`https://universe.bawes.net`), Meet Brick (`/#brick`)
- Facts: Free to start · Works in the browser · No download

## The plaza: "What do you feel like doing?"

Seven spots. Pick one and your avatar walks there; the caption says what it is and where to go. Sprites are the real avatar and Brick sheets from the game (`public/images/home/`).

| # | Spot | Caption title | Caption |
|---|------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | Meet people | Walk up and say hi | Get close to someone and you can hear and see them. Walk away and it goes quiet. It works like a real room. |
| 2 | Make your space | A space that is yours | Start from a ready-made room, move things around, and open the door when you want company. Being alone here is fine too. |
| 3 | Meet the residents | AI residents live here | They walk around, remember you, and talk out loud or in chat. Each one has a job it is good at. |
| 4 | Orbit anywhere | Orbit takes you anywhere | Universes hold worlds, and worlds hold rooms. Orbit shows what is live, keeps your stars and your passport, and takes you there in one tap. |
| 5 | Join in | Talks, classes, game nights | A stage lets one person speak to the whole crowd. Sit in the audience, or take the stage yourself. |
| 6 | Build with Brick | Your own AI companion | Everyone gets a Brick. Chat with it or call it, connect your Apps, and ask it to build things for you. |
| 7 | Share what you make | Apps, pages, documents, drawings | Whatever gets made here is an artifact. Open it inside Universe, or send a link to anyone. |

## Wording rules

- Say "avatar", never "Woka".
- Say "Apps" for connected services.
